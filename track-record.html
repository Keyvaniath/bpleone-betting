    🔮 Prospective Forward-Test — zero hindsight—

    The held-out test above splits the existing ledger. This is stricter: we froze the curation rule on a fixed date and never refit it, then score only picks settled after that date. Every outcome here postdates the rule — there's no way to fit to it. It starts near-empty and builds daily.

    🧾 Methodology Note

    Every surfaced pick is logged and settled against the real result at 1u flat. Picks are graded at the sportsbook price where one exists (DraftKings pre-game; price class A/B). When we find a graded price was wrong, we restate: picks are repriced to the DraftKings pre-game line, and picks no book ever priced are voided — wins and losses alike. Every restatement is dated and disclosed with the record before and after (the price-correction notes above), and the settled history is hashed into the chain shown at the top of this page.

    Player props are graded at the model's own fair odds (class C) because no sportsbook prop prices are recorded; those numbers measure calibration, not betting ROI. The PrizePicks line is an unverified import and is kept separate from every headline. We make no promise of a future hit rate or ROI.
