🧾 Methodology Note

All plays are graded against the price displayed when published. We log the closing line at game start to compute CLV. We do not retroactively remove "bad" plays, change stakes after publication, or hide losing months. The total units shown above include all plays — pre-game, in-game, and live.

Why does this matter? Because most "experts" you'll find online will quietly delete their losses and trumpet their wins. We do the opposite — we log everything precisely so you can validate that the model actually works. A 55-58% hit rate at –110 average vig is the truth of a sharp operation, and that's what we'll deliver if the underlying model is calibrated.
