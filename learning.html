Self-Learning Loop

Every night a job pulls the box scores of every game from yesterday's slate, settles each projection (hit / miss / push), and writes the result to a growing track record. The next morning, a calibration runner re-reads the trailing 30 days, computes per-market bias, Brier score, log loss, and ECE, then writes a correction factor that the next day's projections multiply through. The model only gets smarter.

No corrections fire until a market has at least 30 settled records. Caps at +/- 25% per refresh to avoid runaway adjustment. Day 1: trust the priors. Day 30: trust the data.
