Free ESPN/DK moneyline, de-vigged, vs the model's win prob. Basketball closes sharp — edges are usually small; this is transparency + spotting genuine divergence.
How to read this: Edge is calculated against ACTUAL book prices (live DraftKings lines via ESPN's free feed; frozen snapshot as fallback), not against model fair-odds. A +12% edge means the model thinks $1 on this side returns $1.12 long-run.
Caveat: Very large edges (20%+) often mean the model is overconfident, not that there's free money. The learning loop (learning_integrity + self_learn_weights) shrinks these over time as actual outcomes accumulate.
