🎯 Why CLV matters

Closing line value is a leading indicator: beating the close consistently is evidence of pricing skill, though not proof on its own. CLV needs a real sportsbook price at pick time and at the close, so it exists only for book-priced game lines; props have no book price recorded. Average CLV shows "—" until enough real closing lines accumulate.

🧪 What is in-sample here

This page replays the ledger; it is not a walk-forward backtest. The family curation (cutting market families with n ≥ 25 and net ≤ −5u) and the isotonic recalibration are both fit on the whole settled ledger, so any curated number is in-sample. The out-of-sample checks are separate: the held-out 70/30 split and the frozen-rule forward test on the Track Record — both currently negative.

📦 Production Pipeline

The daily CI pipeline rebuilds the ledger, settles picks against real results and refits two things from the settled outcomes: the per-family isotonic recalibration (model probability → realized hit rate) and the proven-negative family list the boards exclude. Neither is triggered by ROI; both refit every run. Restatements (repricing to DraftKings, voiding unpriced picks) are disclosed on the Track Record.
