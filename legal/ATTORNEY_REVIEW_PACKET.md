# Attorney review packet (Tier 1 #1 — counsel)

Hand this to a **gaming / advertising attorney** so the review is fast and cheap
(you're paying for focused answers, not discovery). EdgeStat is a **sports-analytics
content + affiliate** business — not a sportsbook (we take no wagers, hold no funds).

## What we are
- A static analytics site (betting.bpleone.com) publishing model probabilities,
  projections, and a settled, public track record across ~17 sports.
- Monetization: **affiliate** (sportsbook revenue share) to start; an optional
  **subscription** (Stripe) for premium boards is built but not yet enabled.
- Email newsletter ("The Tape") collected via a form.

## Documents to review (all live on the site)
- Terms of Service — `terms.html`
- Privacy Policy — `privacy.html`
- Disclaimer (results / not-advice) — `disclaimer.html`
- Responsible Gambling — `responsible-gambling.html`
- Affiliate & Advertising Disclosure — `affiliate-disclosure.html`
- Data Sources — `data-sources.html`
- (All contain `[BRACKETED]` placeholders for entity name / state / arbitration body.)

## Specific questions for counsel
1. **Claims substantiation (FTC):** our marketing shows historical ROI / hit-rate /
   "units" with a "past performance ≠ future" disclaimer. Is our substantiation +
   disclaimer placement adequate? Any required changes to how we present the track record?
2. **Paid picks / "tout" rules:** if we enable the subscription (paid premium picks),
   are there state-specific registration or disclosure rules we must follow?
3. **Affiliate compliance:** do any of our target states require **affiliate
   registration** or operator-side disclosure for sportsbook revenue share?
4. **Arbitration / class waiver:** is the clause in `terms.html §12` enforceable in our
   state? Any consumer-law carve-outs to add?
5. **Privacy:** does `privacy.html` match our actual practices under CCPA/CPRA (we
   collect emails + analytics; we don't sell data)? Any GDPR exposure if we have EU users?
6. **Age/geo:** is a 21+ self-attestation age gate sufficient, or do specific states
   require more for affiliate/advertising content?
7. **Data licensing exposure:** we currently use public data feeds (see
   `DATA_LICENSING.md`). What's our risk profile until licensed feeds are in place, and
   which to prioritize?
8. **Entity + insurance:** confirm the LLC structure and whether media/E&O insurance is
   advisable given the performance claims.

## What we've already done
- Full compliance page set, site-wide 21+ age gate + responsible-gambling footer,
  FTC affiliate disclosure, and a published methodology/calibration page (show-your-work).

Budget tip: most of the above is a 1–2 hour consult. Bring the formed LLC details so
the engagement letter and entity references are correct.
