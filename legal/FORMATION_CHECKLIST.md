# LLC formation checklist (Tier 1 #1)

A concrete, do-it-in-an-afternoon checklist to form the entity behind EdgeStat.
This is **general information, not legal or tax advice** — verify current fees/forms
and confirm specifics with an attorney or accountant. Assumes California (you're in
SoCal); a different state changes the forms but not the shape.

> Why it matters: the LLC is the liability shield. Until it exists, you're operating
> a gambling-adjacent content business as an individual — do this before revenue or ads.

## Step 1 — Name + entity
- [ ] Pick a name (e.g. `bpleone LLC` or `EdgeStat LLC`). Check availability on the
      California Secretary of State business search (bizfileOnline.sos.ca.gov).
- [ ] Decide single-member LLC (simplest) vs. multi-member.

## Step 2 — File the formation document
- [ ] File **Articles of Organization (Form LLC-1)** with the CA Secretary of State
      online at bizfileOnline.sos.ca.gov. Filing fee ~$70 (confirm current amount).
- [ ] Designate a **registered agent** for service of process (you, or a paid service
      ~$100–150/yr — a paid agent keeps your home address off the public record).

## Step 3 — Post-formation filings
- [ ] File the **Statement of Information (Form LLC-12)** within 90 days (~$20), then
      every 2 years.
- [ ] Note the California LLC **annual franchise tax** (historically $800/yr to the FTB)
      and any first-year rules — **confirm the current rule with the FTB/your accountant**,
      as these change.

## Step 4 — Federal + banking
- [ ] Get an **EIN** from the IRS — free, online, ~10 min (irs.gov → "Apply for an EIN").
- [ ] Open a **business bank account** in the LLC's name (bring the Articles + EIN).
- [ ] Consider an **operating agreement** — even single-member LLCs should have one.
      Your formation service (e.g. the SoS filing, or LegalZoom/Northwest/your attorney)
      can provide a standard single-member template; don't hand-roll a legal instrument.

## Step 5 — Insurance (recommended for a content site making performance claims)
- [ ] General liability + **media/E&O (errors & omissions)** insurance — media liability
      matters because you publish ROI/track-record claims. Get a quote (Hiscox, Next, etc.).

## Step 6 — Wire the entity into the site
Once formed, fill the placeholders in the legal pages (search the repo for these):
- [ ] `[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]`, `[STATE]`, `[COUNTY, STATE]`, `[MAILING ADDRESS]`,
      `[EFFECTIVE DATE]`, `[DATE]`, `[ARBITRATION BODY]` — in `terms.html`, `privacy.html`,
      etc.
- [ ] Set up role inboxes: `legal@bpleone.com`, `privacy@bpleone.com`, `support@bpleone.com`.

**Then:** hand the finalized pages to the attorney (see `ATTORNEY_REVIEW_PACKET.md`).
