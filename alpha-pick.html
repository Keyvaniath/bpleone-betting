Alpha Pick of the Day

Different from Play of the Day. The Alpha Pick is the model's highest ROI per dollar risked play — could be a favorite or an underdog. POD asks "where am I most confident?" Alpha asks "where is the book most wrong?"

🎯 The One Pick — the single best Alpha play each day, tracked on its own

The full Alpha board surfaces dozens of plays a day — great in aggregate, but hard to follow. This isolates the single highest-EV pick each slate (flat 1u, backfilled from the public ledger, selected on pre-game odds only) so there's one number to watch.

➕ Alpha Props — the pick, plus a diversified prop or two

Want a little more than one bet? This rides the One Pick alongside the highest-EV play in each next distinct prop market — no two plays share a market, so the extras are genuinely different bets, never a re-stamp of the pick. Same honest backfill, flat 1u each.

What each leg adds
Essentially all the edge is in the anchor pick — the diversifier legs run roughly breakeven, so they buy spread, not extra edge.

How the model picks Alpha vs POD

Play of Day ranks by confidence × edge × calibration — bias toward high-probability favorites where the model is sure. Variance-tight. Lower payout but high hit-rate.

Alpha Pick ranks by raw ROI per $1 risked = model_prob × decimal_odds − 1. Excludes longshots below 18% (variance too brutal) and heavy-fav territory above 85% (that's where POD lives). What's left is the band where pricing inefficiency tends to concentrate: solid favs the book is mis-shading or underdogs the model thinks are live.
