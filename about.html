About EdgeStat

EdgeStat is the public face of a private quantitative sports-betting research desk run by Brandon Leone. Built in Southern California. Python, GitHub Actions, a public settled ledger, and a lot of cold coffee.

Our Mission

Most sports bettors lose, and the industry profits from that fact. We think this is largely an information problem. Books have invested in modeling for a decade; bettors haven't. EdgeStat is built to bridge that gap — to give recreational bettors the same quantitative tools the books use, so they can compete on a level field.

We're a research operation first, a publication second. Our outputs — the daily boards, Play of the Day, line-move alerts, the Academy — are how we share what we find. The numbers behind every recommendation are the same numbers we use ourselves.

Founder

Brandon Leone is a former financial analyst (IB / equity research) based in Southern California. He spent half a decade modeling equities and cash flows on Wall Street's clock; now he models baseball games and player props on his own.

If the underlying math of "what's this company worth?" feels eerily similar to "what's this team's true win probability?" — that's because it is. Both are about pricing uncertainty correctly. The sports market is younger and noisier — which is why it is worth measuring carefully, and publishing the result whichever way it goes.

Our Process

Every morning at 6:00 AM ET the data pipeline runs autonomously on GitHub Actions. It (1) settles yesterday's projections against final box scores from MLB Stats API, (2) recomputes per-market calibration metrics on the trailing 30 days, (3) pulls today's schedule, lineups, weather (Open-Meteo), home-plate umpire, and DraftKings odds (The Odds API), (4) layers Baseball Savant Statcast metrics (xK%, xBA, xSLG, barrel%), (5) writes today's fair prices for moneyline, total, and the four player-prop markets, plus a side-by-side PrizePicks comparison.

Two more refreshes at 1 PM ET and 6 PM ET catch line moves and confirmed lineups. The parlay engine combines highest-edge legs into 2- and 3-leg suggestions with same-game correlation haircut. The model bias-corrects per market once 30+ outcomes have flowed -- so day 30 trusts the data; day 1 trusts the priors with caps.

After every game settles, the pick is graded in the all-picks ledger (data/all_picks_ledger.json, kept in git history -- no cherry-picks). When a price or grade turns out wrong, the record is restated and the restatement is disclosed on the track record with before/after numbers (repriced to the DraftKings line, unpriced picks voided). Calibration runs nightly; Brier, log loss, ECE and ROI roll forward continuously.

What we are not

  • Not a tout service. We don't sell "locks." There's no such thing. (The board still called "Locks" is a legacy name for the highest-model-conviction list; its record is public and nothing on it is a lock.) We give you probabilities and rationale; you choose what to bet.
  • Not a sportsbook. We don't take wagers. We don't accept money.
  • Not financial advice. Sports betting is gambling. Bet with money you can afford to lose. 21+ only. 1-800-GAMBLER.
  • Not infallible. Models can be wrong. Variance is real. We publish the receipts whichever way they go — see the track record for where the curated record stands today.

Roadmap

Last updated: November 5, 2026.

  • Shipped: MLB game lines against DraftKings' posted prices; NFL, NBA, NHL and college-football game-line desks with a published verdict each (today: NFL unproven, NBA and NHL no edge, CFB the close is sharper); player-projection models for MLB, NFL, NBA, NHL, WNBA; the calibration loop; a public, hash-chained settled ledger with every price correction disclosed.
  • Not yet: sportsbook prices for player props (the free feed carries none, so every prop on the site is a projection, not a pick); accounts; a paid tier. Nothing is for sale today.
  • The bar for anything new: a pick needs a live DraftKings price and a desk whose validation says it adds something over the market. Most days that is zero picks, and the site says so.
