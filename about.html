About EdgeStat

EdgeStat is the public face of a private quantitative sports-betting research desk run by Brandon Leone. Built in Southern California. Powered by Python, Postgres, and a lot of cold coffee.

Our Mission

Most sports bettors lose, and the industry profits from that fact. We think this is largely an information problem. Books have invested in modeling for a decade; bettors haven't. EdgeStat is built to bridge that gap — to give recreational bettors the same quantitative tools the books use, so they can compete on a level field.

We're a research operation first, a publication second. Our outputs — the daily slate, Play of the Day, sharp flow alerts, the Academy — are how we share what we find. The numbers behind every recommendation are the same numbers we use ourselves.

Founder

Brandon Leone is a former financial analyst (IB / equity research) based in Southern California. He spent half a decade modeling equities and cash flows on Wall Street's clock; now he models baseball games and player props on his own.

If the underlying math of "what's this company worth?" feels eerily similar to "what's this team's true win probability?" — that's because it is. Both are about pricing uncertainty correctly. The sports market is younger, noisier, and less efficient — which is exactly why there's still alpha here.

Our Process

Every morning the data pipeline runs at 6:00 AM ET. It pulls the previous night's box scores, refreshes team true-talent priors (Bayesian shrinkage to league mean), updates pitcher xFIP and lineup wRC+ off Statcast, and re-fits the bullpen rest curves. By 7:00 AM, every game on tonight's slate has a fair price for moneyline, run line, total, first-5, and a handful of player props.

From 7:30 AM until first pitch, we poll six major books every 60 seconds. Anywhere the market disagrees with our number by 2%+ is logged as a potential edge. The biggest, highest-confidence edge becomes the Play of the Day, published by 8:00 AM. Sharp flow alerts fire all afternoon as steam moves hit the wire.

After every game settles, results are logged and the model is re-trained. Hit rate, ROI, CLV all get rolled forward. No revisions. No cherry-picks.

What we are not

  • Not a tout service. We don't sell "locks." There's no such thing. We give you probabilities and rationale; you choose what to bet.
  • Not a sportsbook. We don't take wagers. We don't accept money.
  • Not financial advice. Sports betting is gambling. Bet with money you can afford to lose. 21+ only. 1-800-GAMBLER.
  • Not infallible. Models can be wrong. Variance is real. Long-term, the math has to play out — and we publish the receipts so you can see it does.
