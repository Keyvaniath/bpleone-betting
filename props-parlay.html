Props-Only Parlays

Parlays built from player props only — no game lines mixed in. Many states (and the PrizePicks / Underdog pick'em format) only allow prop-on-prop parlays, so these are legal where mixed parlays aren't. Each is priced off the model: joint hit probability (with a same-game correlation haircut) and the combined payout multiple.
