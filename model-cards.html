Model Cards

One card per sport, the way an ML team documents a model: what the engine is, what it's trained on, how it actually performs, and where it breaks. Performance is computed live from the same public, settled ledger that powers the Strategy Simulator — split by price class and shown both with every family and with the proven-negative families dropped.

ROI is shown only where a sportsbook price exists. Class A/B rows were graded at a DraftKings price, so their ROI is a betting result. Class C rows (every player prop, and most desks) were graded at the model's own fair odds or a PrizePicks break-even: they show hit rate vs the predicted probability, labelled "calibration only" — not a betting ROI. MLB-PP is an unverified PrizePicks import and is not bettable. "Losers dropped" removes the proven-negative families chosen from these same outcomes, so it is in-sample; the held-out test of that cut is negative — see the held-out validation →. Treat anything under ~30 decided bets as a read, not a verdict.
