🎯 Pitcher Same-Game Parlays (SGP) 2–3 correlated bets on one pitcher, combined into a single ticket --
What does "3-LEG" mean? A leg is one bet. A 3-LEG is a same-game parlay that combines 3 outcomes on the same pitcher onto one ticket — e.g. K over 5.5 + ER under 3.5 + Outs over 14.5. Because they're correlated (a dominant start makes all three hit together), we price them with that correlation built in — value a naive parlay calculator misses. The number on the right is the fair combined price.
