ℹ️ How the math works
  • P&L uses the American odds you entered: a win pays stake × (decimal − 1), a loss is −stake, a push is 0.
  • ROI (yield) = net units ÷ units staked on decided bets (pushes excluded from the denominator).
  • CLV compares the implied probability of your price vs the closing price. You beat the close when you locked a longer price than the market settled on — CLV = close_implied − your_implied, in points. Positive is good. It's the sharpest read on whether you're getting value, independent of whether any single bet won.
  • Only settled bets (win/loss/push) count toward record, P&L and ROI. Pending bets show as open exposure.
