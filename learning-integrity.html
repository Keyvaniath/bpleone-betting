How to read this: Each source gets a statistical grade from sample size, calibration, and price. Calibration test: wins vs the model's own predicted probabilities — a small p means the probabilities are wrong, not that there is an edge (hit rate vs a 50% coin flip says nothing: a 75%-predicted prop that hits 70% "beats chance" and still loses at its own price). Edge test: ROI at a real sportsbook price (class A/B rows only; props graded at the model's own odds never count). Grade B = calibrated (Brier<0.24, predictions consistent with outcomes) — honest probabilities, no edge proven. Grade A = 50+ book-priced picks with ROI > 0 at p<0.05, calibrated, CLV not negative. Sources with grade F or D have NO weight changes applied. Universe: working ledger, settled and not voided.
