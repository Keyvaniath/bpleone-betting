📓 How the strategies differ
  • Equal-payout hedge: Stake the hedge so you make the SAME profit regardless of outcome. Eliminates variance but caps upside.
  • Break-even hedge: Stake the hedge so worst case = $0 (no loss either way). Keeps some upside on original side.
  • Optimal Kelly hedge: Given your model probability for the original winning, compute the hedge stake that maximizes expected log bankroll. Often the answer is "don't hedge" if your model still favors the original.
