How the math works
  • Odds: a multiplier like 1.7x is total return per $1 (decimal odds). +328 = 4.28x, −110 = 1.91x.
  • Full hedge = ticket payout ÷ hedge odds. Both sides return the same amount.
  • Break-even hedge = stake ÷ (hedge odds − 1). You lose nothing if your bet misses.
  • Expected value = chance your bet wins × payout − stake + hedge × (chance hedge wins × hedge odds − 1). The last part is the price of the hedge.
  • Live odds on your side strip the vig from both prices to estimate a fair win chance.
  • Pushes on whole-number lines are left out of expected value.
