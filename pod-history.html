📋 What this is

Play of the Day is the single highest-conviction pick the EdgeStat model surfaces each day — the top-EV, slate-confidence-adjusted, walk-forward-validated bet.

Every cron snapshot captures today's POD into a rolling record. Once outcomes settle (via outcomes.py + track_record.json), pending entries flip to WIN/LOSS and contribute to hit-rate, ROI and net-units.

This is the public flagship track record — no edits, no cherry-picks, every pick logged the moment it goes live.
